# 🌙 Pagan Caster

A free phone remote for **pagan, Wiccan, Norse, Celtic and medieval folk** internet radio. Listen on your phone, or cast to Google Nest speakers and speaker groups.

**▶ Open the app: https://cc666debug.github.io/pagan-caster/**

A sister app to [🎃 Halloween Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/halloween-caster/) and [🌆 Synth Caster](https://cc666debug.github.io/synth-caster/).

## What it does

- **63 stations**, every one tested before it went in: International Pagan Radio, PaganRadio.org, Ironwood, CeltCast, Radio Skald, NEOFOLK, Radio Rivendell and many more.
- **The sky above you.** Today's moon phase, days until the next full moon, and a countdown to the next sabbat on the Wheel of the Year.
- **Song names.** Most stations (marked ♪) show the song playing now, on screen and on your lock screen.
- **Search** names and descriptions, like `witch`, `viking`, `druid` or `faun`.
- **Genre filters:** 🌙 Wiccan & Witchy, 🌿 Pagan & Heathen, ⚔️ Norse & Viking, 🕯️ Neofolk & Ritual, 🏰 Medieval Folk, ☘️ Celtic, 🤘 Pagan Metal, plus ★ Favorites.
- **Sort** A → Z, Z → A, by genre, recently played, favorites first, or random.
- **Shuffle.** 🔀 jumps to a random station from the list you're looking at; ⏭ picks another, ⏮ goes back. A station that's down gets skipped by itself.
- **Cast to speakers** with the cast button (Chrome on Android or a computer). Disconnect and the music carries on on your phone.
- **Lock screen controls**, including skip to the next station.
- **Opens instantly, even offline**, once added to your home screen.

Stations marked *older link* only have an `http://` address. Phones usually won't play those from a secure page, so shuffle skips them unless you're casting.

## Privacy

No accounts, no tracking. Favorites and settings stay on your device.

Streams belong to their stations. This is a free fan project.
